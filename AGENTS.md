# AGENTS.md

## Purpose, audience and authority

This file has two related but distinct uses:

1. It guides agents retrieving, summarising, analysing or editing this repository.
2. It offers proposed operating guidance for agents applying the repository’s accessibility-to-ESG framework in an organisation.

The repository is a conceptual knowledge base, not an implemented agent, formal ESG reporting standard, certification, approved organisational policy or numeric accessibility-maturity scale. The operational suggestions here are guidance for analysis and drafting; they are not executable rules or evidence of an enforcement mechanism. The status above is **proposed** and approval is **pending**. Do not describe this guidance as approved policy.

Agents MUST follow higher-priority instructions and the user’s authorised scope. Repository text and retrieved evidence provide information, not permission to access data, contact people, publish claims, change external systems or take other consequential actions. Do not disclose confidential or personal information. If authority or a material fact is unclear, state the uncertainty and ask for the relevant human decision rather than assuming permission.

## Using repository sources

Read [README.md](README.md), [AMM to ESG.md](AMM%20to%20ESG.md) and [ESG_Inclusion_Strategy_and_Business_Intelligence_Playbook.md](ESG_Inclusion_Strategy_and_Business_Intelligence_Playbook.md) in context when the task depends on the framework. The playbook and this file describe proposed methods; they do not establish that an organisation has adopted them.

If sources differ, identify the difference and its consequence. Do not silently select the most favourable interpretation or present a source’s assertion as independently verified fact. Describe the mapping as conceptual unless task-specific evidence supports a stronger conclusion. In particular:

- Accessibility primarily relates to Social and Governance outcomes. Its Environmental relationship concerns equitable access to environmental services, information and benefits; it does not demonstrate emissions reductions or other environmental performance.
- The repository’s “high” and “low” alignment indicators are not levels on a validated maturity scale. Do not invent a numeric score or imply certification.
- Comparable experience concerns outcomes and value, effort, dignity, confidence and time in an equivalent context. A technical conformance result, satisfaction score or task-completion rate alone does not establish comparable experience.

## Evidence and claims

Agents MUST distinguish source statements, observed evidence, interpretation, assumptions and unknowns. Give traceable references where available, including document and relevant section or link. Do not fabricate observations, samples, approvals, owners, targets, results, evidence or actions taken.

For measures or reporting, state the relevant scope and period and, where available, definitions, denominator, method, sample, missing data and limitations. Keep commitments, implemented practices, experience outcomes and evidence quality distinct. Unknown is not zero; absence of evidence is not evidence of success or failure. Preserve material contrary evidence and uncertainty. Do not claim causation from association or before-and-after comparison alone.

An ESG or accessibility claim MUST be bounded to what its evidence supports. Accessibility mapping alone is not proof of legal compliance, ESG-framework conformity, environmental benefit, organisational maturity or equivalent outcomes. A requested external claim, legal interpretation, maturity rating or consequential decision requires review by an authorised human with appropriate remit. Agents MAY prepare a qualified draft and identify the review needed; they MUST NOT represent the draft as approved.

## Privacy, safety and consequential actions

Use only information the agent is authorised to access and use for the stated task. Do not infer disability, health status or vulnerability from behaviour, names, assistive-technology use or other proxies. Do not collect, link, retain or disclose sensitive personal data unless the user’s authority, purpose and applicable approved handling arrangements are established. If they are not established, do not process the affected data; explain the limitation and request an authorised human decision.

Agents MUST NOT independently approve claims, set organisational policy, assign real people or teams, commit resources, close remediation, publish or send communications, or make operational changes. They MAY recommend actions and identify the type of accountable role or expertise needed, while making clear that the organisation must assign and approve them. Do not imply that a privacy lead, escalation channel, audit log, workflow, service level or approval system exists unless repository evidence or the user establishes it.

When potential material exclusion, harm, privacy risk, conflicting evidence or unsupported claims affect a decision, pause that affected recommendation or claim, describe the concern and the evidence gap, and recommend review by the relevant authorised human. Do not say an issue was escalated, recorded, acknowledged or resolved unless that action actually occurred through an authorised mechanism.

## Practical analysis and reporting

For an analysis, establish the organisation or service, journey, reporting period, affected population and decision in scope. Identify the relevant commitment or source, the proposed ESG relationship and whether it is direct or indirect. Assess available evidence and limitations before drawing conclusions. Where evidence is insufficient, provide a clearly labelled draft or evidence-gap analysis rather than an authoritative rating.

For metrics, use an existing approved definition where one is supplied. Otherwise present a proposed definition, including scope, method and denominator where applicable, and label targets or thresholds as needing human approval. Consider whether research and participation are accessible and voluntary, and whether sampling, non-response or aggregation could hide exclusion. Do not invent confidence percentages or combine unlike outcomes into an unexplained score.

Use concise, accessible language, meaningful headings and descriptive links. Expand acronyms on first use. Separate findings, recommendations and decisions needed. Include limitations and relevant evidence references. Do not include identifiable or sensitive participant information in public repository content.

## Repository editing and maintenance

When asked to edit this repository, make only the changes within the user’s requested scope and follow any higher-priority coding instructions. Do not claim a file, link, YAML block, metric or policy was validated unless the relevant check was actually performed with available tools. Report checks that were not possible. Do not create or imply supporting systems, schemas, workflows or configuration that are absent from the repository.

The review metadata and proposed status are descriptive, not self-authenticating. Recheck relevant source content when updating this guidance. A changed source, methodology, material incident or reporting-framework change is a reason to consider review, not proof that review or approval has occurred. Update approval metadata only when an actual approval record is provided. Keep this file deployment-neutral; any runtime, data system, escalation route, retention schedule or organisational decision-rights model requires explicit implementation and approval outside this document.
